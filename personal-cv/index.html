I'm an introvert, and I tend to do my best thinking in quiet environments. Outside of coursework, I write short stories, drabbles, and personal pieces about feelings and experiences, usually written in narrative form. I also enjoy gaming — Stardew Valley and Left 4 Dead 2 are two I keep coming back to.
